## About

Hello, I am Olivier Clerc. I currently work on topics at the intersection of artificial intelligence and education as a Starting Researcher in the Flowers AI & Cognitive Science Lab at Inria Bordeaux (France).

## Current Work

My current work explores how generative AI can support learning, within a collaborative project involving Inria, EvidenceB, and other partners.

Recently, I investigated how middle school students use generative AI in school, and summarized the findings in [this short video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jhuiRymIkyk&t=5s).

I am also currently developing [a web application that uses large language models to automatically annotate and generate textual data](https://github.com/OlivierLClerc/qualitative_analysis_project).

## Previous Research

My background is in cognitive science, and my curiosity has led me to explore a wide range of topics, including:

- [the interaction between language and face perception during infancy](https://theses.hal.science/tel-03828533)
- [communication in bonobos](https://hal.science/hal-01576988v1)
- [emotional responses in cockatiels](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0205314)
- the possibility of inferring internal mental states from subtle facial muscle activity

Alongside my academic work, I have also worked in industry at [Cytoo](https://cytoo.com/), where I developed machine learning models for cellular microscopy image segmentation, contributing to tools used in biomedical research and drug development.
